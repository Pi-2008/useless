# Project Description

**Perfect Spread** is a computer-vision system that measures how much of a slice of bread is covered with chocolate spread using just **one spoon**. A camera captures the bread, analyzes the chocolate coverage, and gives the user a spreading score. The project turns an ordinary breakfast task into a completely unnecessary skill-based challenge, with three levels, character reviews, and sound effects.

### The Problem (that doesn't exist)

Millions of chocolate-spread users face a serious problem: **they don't know how efficiently they can spread one spoon of chocolate.** They often use extra spoonfuls just because parts of the bread remain uncovered. Perfect Spread addresses this extremely important crisis of uneven chocolate distribution.

### The Solution (that nobody asked for)

Perfect Spread uses computer vision to analyze a photograph of the bread and calculate the **percentage covered by chocolate spread**. Users get three difficulty levels, with **Mr. Chocolate and Ms. Chocolate** reviewing their performance. Different coverage ranges trigger different sound effects, making every spreading attempt feel like a dramatic evaluation.

A physical button connected to an **Arduino Nano** captures the image for analysis, turning the software into an interactive physical system.

The structure was created using **repurposed parts**, including a discarded **Sujata juicer** and leftover **WPC pieces from construction work at home**, reducing the need for new materials.
